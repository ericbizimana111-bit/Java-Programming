/// ================== upcasting ================== //

The Real-World AnalogyImagine you have an actual Apple iPhone in your hand.
If someone asks you: "What is that?"You can say: "It is an iPhone." 
(Specific)Or you can say: "It is a Phone." (General)When you call it a "Phone," 
you aren't changing the device itself—it’s still an iPhone—you are just treating it as a general communication tool. 
That is exactly what upcasting is.


// Manually upcasting: You take the Samsung 's' and put it into a 'Callable' variable.

Samsung s = new Samsung();
Callable device = s; 


UPCASTING  is like moving form something that is unique(Samsung) to something that is general common (Phone);
DOWNCASTING is like movign from something that is general(Phone) to something that is unique(Samsung);
